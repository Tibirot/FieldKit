SFA platform · interactive wireframes

Mid-fidelity wireframes of the key flows. The field app is the mobile, offline-first PWA a rep uses in-store; the back office is where ops runs the trade universe. Each screen is tagged with the spec requirements it realizes.

Field app

The golden path, offline

A rep's day, start to finish, with no connectivity in the store. Screens 1–5 run in sequence — sync down, walk in, audit the shelf, take the order, reconcile on reconnect.

Back office

Running the operation

Desktop console for supervisors and sales ops — coverage & compliance at a glance, and the master data that drives the field app. Reporting is the read-side of the modules, not a separate system.
